Six years at the cutting table

About the workshop. ENNI SHOE started in 2019 as a one-table operation, a cutting knife, offcuts of leather, and a promise to never rush a stitch. That promise hasn't changed, even as the orders have grown.

We build for people who notice the difference between a shoe that was assembled and one that was made the give in a full-grain upper, the weight of a proper sole, the fit of a last that was actually tested on feet. Every pair that leaves our table has been through the same hands, start to finish.

The process

Cut

Full-grain hide is hand-marked and cut to pattern, grain direction checked panel by panel.

Skive

Edges are thinned so seams sit flat instead of bulking under the stitch line.

Stitch

Panels are hand-guided through the machine no shortcuts on seam allowance.

Last

The upper is pulled and set over a wooden last to take its final shape.

Finish

Sole is set, edges burnished, leather conditioned before it's boxed.
