Before you order

Questions:

How long does a custom pair take?

In-stock styles ship within 2-3 days. Custom sizing or colour requests take 7-10 working days since each pair is cut to order.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?

Yes, we ship nationwide via courier. Delivery fees depend on location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

Can I get a size exchanged?

Unworn pairs in original packaging can be exchanged for a different size within 5 days of delivery. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange it.

How do I place a bulk or corporate order?

Use the contact form above with your quantity and style, or message us directly "we quote bulk orders separately".
